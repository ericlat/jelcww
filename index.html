About Us

JEL Custom Woodworks is a small-batch woodworking studio dedicated to crafting heirloom-quality furniture and custom pieces. Every item is handcrafted using sustainably sourced hardwoods — from white oak and walnut to reclaimed barn timber. No shortcuts, no flat-pack. Just honest, lasting work made the old way.
