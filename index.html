About Us

From a family of craftsmen, my passion for woodworking began with my grandfather. Working with him and my uncle at a young age showed me the value of hard work and cemented my love for creating with wood. Today, rooted in Nashville, TN, we make custom cabinetry, furniture, and unique items. We handle design, planning, fabrication and installation -- every detail with care to bring your idea to life. Honest, lasting work built with thoughtfulness, made for your space.

If it's made of wood, we work with it.
